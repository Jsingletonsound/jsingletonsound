# James Singleton

Emmy-nominated sound designer and sound supervisor. Ten years in audio post-production for TV animation, now working on where AI fits into professional audio. Carlsbad, CA (San Diego).

- **Head of Sound Design, Margarita Mix (FotoKem), 2025–2026.** Managed the entire sound design department and was the first contact for every sound design client: scoped and bid each project, then supervised the work through final mix and stem delivery.
- **Audio Lead (contract), Meta, 2026.** Led audio on a generative AI video project: designed sound effects, wrote the prompts users call them up with, and reviewed model output against a TV and film standard.
- **Associate Faculty, Music Technology, MiraCosta College.** Teaches acoustics, audio electronics, sound for visual media, and digital audio production.
